Boston Institutional Records

A database of the children who passed through Boston's 19th-century institutions..

Explore the Database

This project brings together admission records from institutions that cared for orphaned, indigent and vulnerable children in 19th century Boston. It currently draws on records from the Boston Female Asylum, Dorchester Industrail School for Girls, New England Home for Little Wanderers and Charles St. Temporary Home. The goal of this project is to take these records and present them in a cohesive and connected format. The database acts as a way to filter, investigate, and explore the lives of children who spent time at these institutions, and trace their journeys.

While this project began out of practical necessity for my own dissertation research, I hope this will be able to support wider access to the material and encourage discussion about Boston's institutitonal past.

The database is a work in progress, as part of my Digital Humanities Certification at Northeastern Unviersity.
